
1. Customer

Customer (Customer_ID)
PK: Customer_ID


 2. Demographics

Demographics (Customer_ID, Gender, Age, Under_30, Senior_Citizen,Married, Dependents, Number_of_Dependents)
PK: Customer_ID
FK: Customer_ID → Customer(Customer_ID)(1:1 relationship → FK also serves as PK, no separate ID needed)

3. Location

Location (Location_ID, Customer_ID, Address, City, Zip_Code, Latitude, Longitude)
PK: Location_ID
FK: Customer_ID → Customer(Customer_ID)
FK: Zip_Code → Population(Zip_Code)

4. Population
Population (ID, Zip_Code, Population)
PK: ID
Candidate Key: Zip_Code (unique, referenced by Location)

 5. Services

Services (Service_ID, Customer_ID, Referred_a_Friend, Number_of_Referrals,Tenure_in_Months, Offer, Phone_Service, Avg_Monthly_Long_Distance_Charges,Multiple_Lines, Internet_Service, Internet_Type, Avg_Monthly_GB_Download,Online_Security, Online_Backup, Device_Protection_Plan, Premium_Tech_Support,Streaming_TV, Streaming_Movies, Streaming_Music, Unlimited_Data,Contract, Paperless_Billing, Payment_Method, Monthly_Charge,Total_Charges, Total_Refunds, Total_Extra_Data_Charges,Total_Long_Distance_Charges, Total_Revenue)
PK: Service_ID
FK: Customer_ID → Customer(Customer_ID)

 6. Status

Status (Status_ID, Customer_ID, Satisfaction_Score, Customer_Status,Churn_Label, Churn_Score, CLTV)
PK: Status_ID
FK: Customer_ID → Customer(Customer_ID)
