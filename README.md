# Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn in the telecommunications industry using SQL, Python, Excel, and Power BI. The goal is to identify the factors affecting customer churn and provide business recommendations based on data analysis.

---

## Project Objectives

- Clean and preprocess the dataset.
- Analyze customer behavior.
- Identify the main factors affecting customer churn.
- Build interactive dashboards.
- Generate business insights.
- Provide recommendations to improve customer retention.

## Key Insights

- Customers with month-to-month contracts have the highest churn rate.
- Customers using Fiber Optic internet are more likely to churn.
- Long-term customers have a lower churn rate.
- Electronic Check is the most common payment method among churned customers.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Encourage customers to switch to long-term contracts.
- Improve customer support for Fiber Optic users.
- Launch loyalty programs for long-term customers.
- Offer personalized retention offers for customers at high risk of churn.
